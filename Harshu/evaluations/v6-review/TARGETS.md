## Corrections to the previous reasoning

- Selected Trauma losses were treated as the leading global defect despite 11/14
  wins there and 9/11 on Queen. These maps are preservation checks; Trophy and
  Slithery are primary improvement targets. Aggregates mix opponents/ranked status.
- Several changes were altered together and inferred from tiny fixed-seed results.
  Those runs do not identify which individual feature helped or harmed.
- A safe simulated gift does not mean the actual recipient chooses it. V6 requires
  receiver agreement and then collection priority, not a copied hypothetical brain.
- Ordinary status sonar isn't coordination; receipt, post-move origin, message age,
  ID order, forced motion and the actual sacrificed tile must all be represented.
- A farm point is an area with circulation and approaches, not a place all workers
  die regardless of receiver presence. Donations cost roughly half their length.
- Geometric center occupancy is descriptive. The bot must use seen topology and
  actual paths; it cannot know hidden terrain or replay food timers.
