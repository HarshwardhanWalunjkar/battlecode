## Operational details

Your team keeps one colour throughout a battle. Autoscrim selection prefers opponents not encountered recently and may provide fewer matches when candidates run out. Submissions cannot be deleted; only one is active.

## Inference

Use unranked selected-map battles to isolate weaknesses; use broad local evaluation before activation. Disabling manually requested ranked matches does not suspend autoscrims. Publication of battle replays means surprises may be short-lived.
