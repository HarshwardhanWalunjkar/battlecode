## Architecture decisions being evaluated

1. Preserve body/portal pathfinding. Add bounded rescue planning for sequential
   splits only when ordinary routes fail; preserve maximum rear rescue of the coil.
2. Opening pressure must reflect actual replay attacks and food/worker losses,
   not blindly increase suicide trades or target one opponent.
3. Stable local feeding areas, assignment persistence, and matching safety checks
   for agreeing to a meal and collecting it. No sacrifice on an unsupported promise.
4. Child education uses post-action ray/body delivery, not head alignment: every
   split retains the old tail as the new head. Survival and rescue roles outrank intel.
5. Bounded terrain and portal sharing as navigation hints, never as proof of safe
   occupancy/escape; direct sight overrides reports. Current compact 4x4 proposal
   lacks portal IDs and strong framing, so use smaller independently valid packets.
6. Food reports are aged targets, not invented edible pearls. Never assume silence
   proves isolation. Quiet-front exploration may change, collision checks stay exact.
